# pairly-website

Landing page for **Pairly**, the study-group app for university students.

Owner: Wop testwell (Project Manager)

## Goals for the beta launch (Nov 2)

- Explain Pairly in one sentence: *"Find your study group in under a minute."*
- Collect beta sign-ups from Westbrook University students
- Show quotes from the first beta testers (collected by Tomas Rivera, our Westbrook campus ambassador)
- Link to the Android and iOS store pages once they are approved
